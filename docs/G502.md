# The original G502 (Proteus Core)

The first G502, sold as the G502 Proteus Core, calls itself "Tunable FPS Gaming Mouse G502". It's wired, USB 046D:C07D. The facts here come from a real one (firmware U 88.02, build 0017, [reports/g502-c07d.txt](reports/g502-c07d.txt)) unless a source is named.

## Features

0x2201 DPI (50 to 12000 in steps of 50), 0x8060 report rate (125, 250, 500 and 1000 Hz), 0x8100 onboard profiles, 0x8110 button spy, 0x1300 LED control, 0x2230 angle snapping (fn 0 answers 00: off), 0x2240 surface tuning (fn 0 answers `01 34 E5 01 34 E5 00 0A 10`, layout unknown) and hidden ones (0x18A1, 0x1E00, 0x1E20, 0x1EB0, 0x1850, 0x1801, 0x1802, 0x00C1). No 0x8070: its lights are 0x1300 only.

## Onboard profiles: format 1

0x8100 fn 0 answers `01 01 01 03 03 0B 10 01 00 0A 01`: memory model 1, profile format 1, macro format 1, 3 profiles, 3 factory profiles, 11 buttons, 16 sectors of 256 bytes (bytes 7–8 `01 00`; the newer mice have 255).

Format 1 has format 3's layout up to 0xD0, which is everything G-Lite writes (libratbag and g502-hub read formats 1 to 5 alike there). The report bears it out:

- byte 0 the report interval in ms (`01`, 1000 Hz), byte 1 the default stage (`01`: 2400, the DPI it ran at), byte 2 the shift stage;
- five stages from byte 3, little-endian (1200, 2400, 3200, 6400, then 0 for none);
- 11 buttons of 4 bytes from 0x20, the G-shift layer from 0x60, the name in UTF-16 at 0xA0 ("Profil 1", set by Logitech Gaming Software in French);
- nothing from 0xD0 (all FF), and the CRC-16 in the last two bytes, valid on the three profiles and the directory.

The directory (sector 0x0000) is Logitech Gaming Software's: `00 01 01 00`, `00 02 01 00`, `00 03 01 00`, then `FF FF 00 00`. The factory copies at 0x0101–0x0103 have no CRC (FF FF), like the G Pro Wireless's, and 0x0101 and 0x0102 match profiles 1 and 2. 0x0100 isn't a profile: it starts `01 01 01 FF 01 02 01 FF 01 03 01 FF FF FF`, then `U88.03_B0017` and tables.

G-Lite saves it like formats 4 and 5 (`State.ClassicFormat`): the edited stage, the default stage, the report interval and the buttons, never the bytes from 0xD0. Restore the factory profile copies the profile's own factory copy (profile 3 from 0x0103). The three profiles show as P1 to P3 in the footer. Tried on the mouse (2026-11-27): a stage saved to P1 (the DPI buttons then cycled through it), 500 Hz saved to P3 and back, P3's bindings kept, P1 to P3 switched from G-Lite and from G9, and P2 restored from 0x0102.

## Buttons

Eleven, the G502 HERO's slots (`Bindings.Names`): left, right, wheel click, thumb back (G4) and front (G5), sniper, G7, G8, G9, wheel tilt right and left. Profile 1's bindings are the HERO's factory ones: `90 07` DPI shift on the sniper, `90 04` and `90 03` DPI down and up on G7 and G8, `90 0A` profile cycle on G9, `90 02` and `90 01` the tilts. g502-profile-manager (MIT) uses the same map for every G502.

## Lights: 0x1300

Three LEDs, read with fn 0, 1, 2, 4 and 6 and decoded with libratbag's `hidpp20.h` (MIT: types 1 battery, 2 DPI, 3 profile, 4 logo; mode bits 01 off, 02 on, 04 blink, 08 travel, 10 ramp up, 20 ramp down, 40 heartbeat, 80 breathing):

| Index | fn 1 (info) | Type | Physical | Modes | fn 4 (state) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `00 02 03 00 03 07` | DPI | 3 | off, on | `00 01 00 00`: off |
| 1 | `01 03 03 00 03 00` | profile | 3 | off, on | `01 01 00 00`: off |
| 2 | `02 04 01 00 80 00` | logo | 1 | breathing | `02 80 00 FF`: lit steadily |

Tried on the mouse (2026-11-27):

- **Software control:** fn 2 answers 00 as the mouse comes; fn 5 is refused (error 02) until fn 3 `01` takes control. So 01 is software control, as libratbag's driver has it (its header says the opposite).
- **Setting a state:** fn 5 takes the LED, the mode as two bytes big-endian (`00 80`), then the parameters. For breathing: the brightness as two bytes, then the period in ms as two bytes (`02 00 80 00 64 0B B8`). One mode byte (`02 02 00`) is refused. fn 4 reads back in another layout (`02 80 00 FF`), not decoded.
- **The logo** takes only breathing, as fn 1 says, and it's enough: breathing with a period of 0 is a steady light (it read `02 80 00 FF` lit steadily, and writing brightness FF with period 0 gave the same), and a brightness of 0 turns it off. Brightness and speed both showed. G-Lite offers Off, Static and Breathe, brightness taken as 0 to 255.
- **The DPI and profile LEDs** take only off: on (`00 02`), with any parameter tried, is refused.
- **Handing control back** (fn 3 `00`): the logo keeps what was set. While G-Lite holds control the DPI lights stop showing the level when the DPI changes (the profile light still shows), so control goes back to the mouse a second after the last change; the logo blinks once then. Handed back after each change instead, the logo flickered while a slider moved.
- The profile has no room for it (nothing from 0xD0), so whether the mouse keeps it once unplugged isn't known; G-Lite keeps the light (`lighting.<device>` in `settings.txt`, as for the G305) and sends it again when the screen loads.
