# G213

"Gaming Keyboard G213", wired, USB 046D:C336, HID++ 2.0 at index 0xFF. Firmware U1 09.00 build 0006. From its report (G-Lite 0.0.26-alpha, 2026-11-15, [reports/g213-c336.txt](reports/g213-c336.txt)).

Its name puts the model last, so `FoundDevice.LooksG` accepts "Gaming Keyboard" (or Mouse, Headset) before a G model, and `ShortName` drops it. It gets the 0x8070 keyboard screen (`ZonesPage`, `FoundDevice.IsLedKeyboard`). See [keyboards-8070.md](keyboards-8070.md).

## Lighting (0x8070 v5)

Six zones:

| Zone | Location | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 (primary): the whole keyboard | Off, Static, Breathe (0x0A), Cycle (0x03), Wave (0x04) |
| 1–5 | 6–10 (primary 1–5): five areas | Off, Static |

The screen names them Keys and Zone 1 to Zone 5, from the location, and greys out the effects a zone doesn't list. Wave is sent with OpenRGB's 0x04 layout from [G815-G915.md](G815-G915.md) (period low at 6, direction 7, intensity 8, period high 9) and the G515's directions (6 left, 1 right, 3 centre out, 8 centre in), checked on the keyboard.

The keyboard has two modes: one effect on zone 0 for the whole keyboard, or a colour per area. Setting an area ends zone 0's effect on every area, and setting zone 0 covers the areas. So the zone row shows All and Zone 1 to 5 (no Keys: on the G213 it's what All sets), and setting one area turns the other areas that followed zone 0 into Static in zone 0's colour (Off stays Off), and sends them.

## Key picture

`models/g213.json` (`"kind": "zones"`) holds each key's zone in `ids`, not a lighting id. When 0x4540 reports a layout of 2 or more (`KeyLayout.IsIso`; this keyboard reports 0x0D), the screen shows the ISO picture `KeyLayout.Iso` builds from it. A key shows its area's light when the area lists that effect, else zone 0's. Clicking a key picks its zone.

Zones as seen on the keyboard with each area in its own colour: 1 up to the 4, R, F, V column, 2 up to 9, O, K and the comma, 3 the rest of the main block, 4 the arrows block and the keys above it, 5 the numpad and the media keys above it. The function row, the bottom row and the top keys are drawn from the product's layout, and are mostly right on the keyboard: Esc to F3 zone 1, F4 to F8 zone 2, F9 to F12 zone 3, the space bar zone 2.

Checked on the keyboard (2026-11-15):

- All → Static lights the whole keyboard.
- Zones 1–5 are the five areas from left to right. They stay Zone 1 to Zone 5.
- An effect on zone 0 replaces the colours set on zones 1–5: with Zone 3 set to a colour, All → Breathe breathes the whole keyboard.
- The other way round, an area stops it: All → Breathe, then Zone 2 → Static red leaves Zone 2 red and the rest stops breathing. With G-Lite sending the other areas as Static in zone 0's colour, the rest is steady in the Breathe colour, as the picture shows.
- Wave works, with all four directions and the speed.
- The picture's zones are right; the guessed rows (function row, bottom row, top keys) are good enough for now.

No 0x8040 (brightness): Static scales the colour, Breathe and Cycle take an intensity byte. No onboard profiles.

## The light button

The button above Pause (a sun) turns the backlight off and on inside the keyboard. Checked on the keyboard (2026-11-16), with a probe listening to the HID++ collections (COL03 long, COL04 short) and the consumer collection (COL02) while the button was pressed:

- Nothing is reported on any of them.
- 0x8070 fn 12 (getCurrentColor, zone 0) keeps answering the colour that was set (`FF 20 20`) while the light is off.
- Lighting sent while it's off (fn 3, zone 0 Static `FF 20 20`, RAM) is accepted and stays dark.

So G-Lite can't see the off state or undo it, and neither can G HUB (checked the same day). The screen's status line says to press the button when the keys stay dark. Logitech's 0x8070 spec (v7) has no on/off and no event for it either; the keyboard has no 0x8040 or 0x1982.

The picture's top row: the game mode button above Print Screen, the light button above Pause, mute and the volume rocker above the numpad; play/pause, stop, back and forward in the function row above the numpad. The numpad's + and Enter are two rows tall (`+:1:2`: a key taller than a row hangs into the next one).

## Saving

It doesn't keep lighting over a power cycle. Tested 2026-11-15 with G-Lite closed: zone 0 Static purple with persistence 1 (RAM and EEPROM), then Static green with persistence 0, both accepted. After unplugging and plugging in, it showed its own Cycle, not purple or green. This agrees with the report: every zone's persistency capabilities (fn 1 byte 4) are 0. fn 9 (read the effect, source 0 or 1) answers all zeros, even for the lighting that's on. G HUB can't save it either: with G HUB closed, the keyboard came back with Cycle (checked the same day). So there's no Save: G-Lite sets the lighting again when it starts and when the keyboard comes back.
