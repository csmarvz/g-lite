# G560 Gaming Speaker

USB 046D:0A78. It speaks HID++ 2.0 at index 0xFF. G-Lite opens it on `PageSpeaker` with live lighting only: Off, Static, Breathe and Cycle, a colour, brightness and speed, on all four zones at once or on each one (bottom left, bottom right, top left and top right: an owner checked that zones 1 to 4 are these, in that order). G-Lite keeps the lighting as `lighting.<device>` in `settings.txt` (one `effect,colour,period,brightness` per zone, separated by `;`) and sets it again on each load. Not checked on real speakers yet.

It also has a Screen effect, like the keyboards': four areas of the screen (by default bottom left, bottom right, top left and top right, as in G HUB's Screen Sampler), area n giving its colour to zone n, sent live as Static with 0x8070 fn 3 (12 bytes, so not stored) only when a colour changes, and at least every 2 s. Monitor, brightness (each colour is scaled before it's sent, like Static), colour boost, update rate, smoothing and the pause when the screen is off or locked are kept as `screen.<device>`, and `screen.on.<device>=1` while it runs, like the keyboards.

## What the report shows

- Features: 0x8070 (LED effects, like the mice), 0x8040 (brightness), 0x8010 (G keys, count 1), 0x8310 (equalizer, all bands 0), and 0x8320 and 0x8305, which G-Lite doesn't know.
- 0x8070 has four zones. fn 1 gives each one a location (bytes 1–2: 1, 2, 3, 4) and five effects. G HUB names the G560's zones left and right, front and back; which location is which isn't known here.
- The effects, the same on every zone: index 0 id 0x00 (Off), 1 id 0x01 (Static), 2 id 0x03 (Cycle, parameters `C0 05 03 E8`), 3 id 0x07 (audio visualizer, `00 67 03 E8`), 4 id 0x0A (Breathe, `C1 05 00 3C`). Static, Cycle and Breathe have the same ids and descriptions as on the G Pro Wireless, so G-Lite sends them with `GMouse.Encode`. 0x07 is the audio visualizer in the 0x8070 effect table (`docs/lighting-control.md`); no source gives its parameters, so it isn't offered.
- 0x8040: max 100 (0x64), now 100. G-Lite leaves it alone and scales the light like on the mice.

## G HUB

G HUB's lighting screen for the G560 (a screenshot from the owner) has two tabs, FRONT and BACK, and a Screen Sampler with four samples: Bottom left, Bottom right, Top left and Top right. So the four zones are probably the front and back lights of the left and right speakers.

## Not yet

- Effect 0x07, the audio visualizer: its parameters.
- The equalizer (0x8310). Other devices give the band count and dB range on fn 0 and the frequencies on fn 1 (7 per call); reports now read both. No source shows a write that's heard, and the PRO X stores its equalizer without applying it. Next: the owner's report, then one write to try.
- 0x8320 (named "headset out" by Solaar), 0x8305 (unnamed anywhere found) and the G key (0x8010).
- Whether the speakers keep the lighting after they lose power. G-Lite sets it again anyway.
