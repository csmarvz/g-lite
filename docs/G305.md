# G305 LIGHTSPEED

What the G305 (and G304: same wireless id, 4074) showed when tried with G-Lite on 2026-11-19. Its report is [reports/g305-4074.txt](reports/g305-4074.txt).

## Lighting is live only

The G305 lists 0x8070 with one zone (Off, Static, Cycle, Breathe), and its one LED takes effects, colour and brightness live. But it ignores the lighting record of its onboard profile: after Save to mouse wrote Static at 0xD0 and the profile was loaded again (0x8100 fn 3), the LED went off. libratbag lists no LEDs for the G305.

So G-Lite treats its lighting as live only (`FoundDevice.LightIsLiveOnly`): it keeps it as `lighting.<device>`, leaves the profile's record alone on Save, and sends it again:

- when the mouse screen loads (also after a Save, whose reload turns the LED off);
- when a scan remakes the screen, which happens after the mouse is switched off: `DevicePage.SendsAgain` makes the new screen read the mouse even when it isn't shown;
- when the mouse sends 0x1D4B event 0 (wireless device status). It did after being switched off and on (the log says "woke up"), not after a sleep.

The LED goes off by itself after about 90 s without use, whatever the profile says, and comes back on with the mouse. That isn't the mouse going to sleep.

## Idle times: no effect seen

Bytes 0x1C–0x1F of a profile hold two times in seconds, little-endian (g502-hub's protocol notes call them timeouts; on the G915 TKL they are power save and off, confirmed). The G502 X ships `3C 00 2C 01` there (60, 300); the G305, G Pro Wireless and G502 LIGHTSPEED have `FF FF FF FF`.

Written on the G305 (with a backup, checksum fixed, profile loaded again):

- 30 + 60: the LED went off at about 90 s, which first looked like the mouse reading the bytes.
- 15 + 45, 15 + 105, 60 + 240: the LED still went off at about 90 s.
- 30 + 30, then 3 minutes untouched: no 0x1D4B event on moving the mouse, while switching it off and on just before had sent one.

So nothing shows the G305 using these bytes. Either it ignores them, or waking from sleep doesn't send the event; this test can't tell those apart. G-Lite doesn't offer them on mice. The G Pro Wireless is still to try, the same way, judging by the 0x1D4B event and not by the LED.
