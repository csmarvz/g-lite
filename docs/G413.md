# G413 Silver

"G413 Silver Mechanical Gaming Keyboard", wired, USB 046D:C33A, HID++ 2.0 at index 0xFF. Firmware U1 14.00 build 0008. From the maintainer's keyboard, Swiss layout ([reports/g413-c33a.txt](reports/g413-c33a.txt), 2026-11-22). The G413 Carbon has the same USB ID and red LEDs; only the Silver's report is known.

`ShortName` gives "G413 Silver" (it drops "Mechanical Gaming Keyboard", with or without "RGB"). It gets the 0x8070 keyboard screen (`ZonesPage`, `FoundDevice.IsLedKeyboard`). See [keyboards-8070.md](keyboards-8070.md).

## Features

0x8070 v5 (lighting), 0x8060 (report rate: 125–1000 Hz, `8B`, now 1 ms), 0x4540 (layout `0D`, ISO), 0x4522 (the feature behind the G815's and G915's Game mode key list, see `GameMode.cs`), 0x40A2, 0x1BC0, 0x1821, 0x00C2, and the hidden 0x1E00, 0x1EB0, 0x1801, 0x1802, 0x1806. No 0x8071, 0x8081, 0x8040 (brightness) or 0x8100 (profiles).

## Lighting (0x8070 v5)

- fn 0: `01 00 01 00 08`. One zone; non-volatile capabilities 0x0001 (bit 0, boot-up effect); extended capabilities 0x0008: bit 3, **monochrome only** ([lighting-control.md](lighting-control.md)). The G213 (RGB) and the G560 leave bit 3 clear.
- Zone 0, location 1 (the keys), three effects: Off (0x00), Static (0x01, capabilities 0x0005) and Breathe (0x0A, capabilities 0xC001, period 0x03E0). No Cycle or Wave.

G-Lite reads bit 3 in `ZoneLighting.Zones`. On a monochrome keyboard `ZonesPage` hides the colour swatches, says in the colour row that Brightness sets the level, and sends every light white (`FF FF FF` for Static at 100%, `7F 7F 7F` at 50%): every channel the same, whichever the keyboard uses. G HUB shows a slider from black to white for this keyboard (`FCFCFC` at its top), which is the same thing. A preset's colour is replaced by white when it's sent. Static and Breathe were tried on the keyboard (2026-11-22).

- Breathe's brightness did nothing on the keyboard: it ignores the intensity byte (param 6). g810-led sends Breathe with that byte as the period's low byte, usually 0, so it never relied on it either. G-Lite scales Breathe's white by the brightness instead (`7F 7F 7F` at 50%).
- With one zone, the zone row (All, Keys) is hidden and the rest of the box moved up (`ZonesPage.HideZoneRow`).
- g810-led sends a single-colour keyboard's level in red only (`II0000`, for the G610 and G413) and lists the G413 as an intensity keyboard. Whether the white Silver reads red or the brightest channel isn't known; white sends every channel.

## Key picture

`models/g413.json`, from a drawing and a photo of the keyboard sent by its owner: full size, the numpad + and Enter two rows tall, the keys with their lighting ids (usage minus 3, as `g-full.json`), which the zones screen lights all as zone 0 (a model without `"kind": "zones"`) and the Game view uses to pick keys. Above the numpad, the two indicator lights, Ⓐ (caps lock) and Ⓖ (Game mode), drawn as small discs, and the G logo at the right; none of the three is lit by the backlight (id `--`). The Swiss keyboard gets the ISO picture (`models/iso/g413-iso.json`).

## Fn keys

From the owner, printed under the keys: F7 lighting, F8 Game mode (its light is Ⓖ), F9 play/pause, F10 stop, F11 previous, F12 next, PrtSc mute, ScrLk volume down, Pause volume up. The picture doesn't show them.

## Audio

G HUB has an audio visualizer for this keyboard. G-Lite's Audio effect (`ZoneLighting.Audio`, id 0xE2 like the per-key one, offered on zones keyboards wherever a zone lists Static) does the same with one level: every 50 ms the loudest of eight bands from `AudioLoopback` (30% to 90% of its range spread over the whole level) sets the light, rising at once and falling at 1.6 a second, sent as Static (white here) in steps of 2% when it moves and every 2 s (`ZonesPage.AudioTick`). Brightness is its top level. Not tried on the keyboard yet.

## Game mode

Fn+F8 turns Game mode on (the Ⓖ light). The keyboard has 0x4522 v0, the version the G815 and G915 have, so `ZonesPage` gets the same Game view (`GameModeBox`, shared with `KeyboardPage`): the keys picked are sent with fn 3 then fn 1, kept in settings and presets, and sent again when the keyboard connects. Marked experimental: not tried on the G413. Neither g810-led nor logi-control-panel covers it.

## Bindings

G HUB's bindings on this keyboard are the F keys remapped by G HUB itself: they stop working when G HUB is closed (checked by the owner, 2026-11-22). Nothing to do on the keyboard.

## Other effects

The keyboard lists Off, Static and Breathe only. Effects that change colour (Cycle, Wave) don't fit one colour, and per-key effects need 0x8080 or 0x8081, which it doesn't have. Other whole-keyboard effects G-Lite draws, like Audio, are possible.

## Saving

g810-led's `-fx-store` sets byte 16 of fn 3 (the byte after the ten parameters) to 1, "user-stored lighting", and offers it on the G413 (`userstoredlighting`); fn 0's non-volatile capabilities on this keyboard are 0x0001 (boot-up effect). The G213 accepts the same byte and stores nothing ([G213.md](G213.md)). Saving on the G413 is untried.
